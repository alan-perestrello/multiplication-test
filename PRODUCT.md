# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A child of about 7 to 9 who answers multiplication questions alone, usually on a tablet with touch, and a parent who sets up the test and reads the report card. The child is mid-task and needs the question and answer box to be the only thing that matters. The parent wants to see progress and weak times tables without digging.

## Product Purpose

A single-page times table test that builds speed and accuracy through short, repeatable practice. It gives immediate feedback per question, saves results per child in the browser, and shows progress over time (accuracy, speed, weakest tables). Success is a child who chooses to do another round and a parent who can see what to practise next.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, encouraging, calm. Warm wording and small moments of praise, but uncluttered and low pressure.

## Anti-references

- Childish cartoon style: mascots, primary colour clutter, comic fonts.
- Pressure and shame: red crosses, countdown panic, harsh failure messages.
- School worksheet look: plain, grey, test paper feel.
- Corporate SaaS dashboard: dense cards, metric tiles, analytics tone.

## Design Principles

1. The question is the screen. Nothing competes with the sum and the answer box during a test.
2. Wrong is information, not failure. Feedback shows the right answer without alarm or blame.
3. Progress should be felt. Show improvement against the child's own earlier results, not against a bar.
4. Built for fingers. Large targets, no hover dependence, no reliance on a physical keyboard.
5. Parent detail stays out of the child's way. Settings and analysis live behind clear, separate screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, full keyboard operation and reduced motion support. Primarily used on tablets with touch, so touch targets and on-screen keypad behaviour matter. Do not rely on colour alone to show right and wrong.
